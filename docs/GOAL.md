# Goal

## Problem

AI and machine learning students need a clear way to discuss the distance between the fictional surveillance system in *Person of Interest* and real data, analytics, and AI capabilities. A 40-minute oral lesson needs visuals that support the presenter and invite decisions and discussion.

## Intended outcome

A complete, offline-capable interactive web presentation titled **From Person of Interest to real AI-surveillance**, with 12 scenes and a closing scene, runs from `index.html` in a browser.

## Users / stakeholders

The presenter and students in a Swedish vocational higher education AI/ML program. All presentation and repository README text is in English.

## Success conditions

- All scenes, reveals, diagrams, quiz questions, and discussion interactions work from local files.
- A presenter can navigate by keyboard or visible controls at 1920×1080 and 1366×768 without clipped content or horizontal scrolling.
- Missing optional copyrighted media shows a usable fallback.
- The factual comparison separates fictional concepts, real platforms, and an invented case study.

## Non-goals

- No backend, live data collection, real surveillance, or actual AI inference.
- No copyrighted film or images are bundled.
- No mandatory network request during the lesson.

## Open questions

- Whether the owner will add licensed media before public release.
